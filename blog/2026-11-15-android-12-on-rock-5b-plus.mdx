I got a Radxa ROCK 5B+ (RK3588) to learn Android below the app layer. This is the short version of getting Android 12 running on it: from the first boot to my own build running from an NVMe SSD.

{/* truncate */}

## What I did

- **Booted the official Radxa OS from a microSD card** to check that the board, power and display work.
- **Flashed Android 12 to an NVMe SSD** from a Linux PC, with the board in Maskrom mode, using Linux_Upgrade_Tool.
- **Built Android 12 from source**, first from Radxa's manifests and later from my own [Gitea server](/blog/self-hosting-android-source-with-gitea).
- **Enabled NVMe boot** in the board configuration, flashed my own `update.img`, and confirmed from the kernel command line that it boots from NVMe.

## What tripped me up

- The packaged `rkdeveloptool` did not work for me. Linux_Upgrade_Tool did.
- The build needed `libncurses5` and Python 2 (for U-Boot) installed by hand, and the kernel only built with GCC after Clang failed.
- Moving the source tree left stale paths in the build output, which broke the build until I deleted the stale `m4` symlink.
